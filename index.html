Project Intake, Assessment & Decision Support

A practical tool I built for better project intake, assessment and delivery decisions.

Work through the request one section at a time. Examples help clarify what useful answers look like; your progress is saved in this browser, and the final assessment separates blockers from follow-up items.

    This page runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Answers are saved in this browser only, so they are still here if you refresh. Use Start over to clear them. Checks use simple pattern matching. Decision-support content is generated from the answers entered and is intended to guide human review, not replace it or authorize delivery.
